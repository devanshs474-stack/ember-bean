Our Roots & Craft

Honoring Every Bean From Soil to Cup

EMBER & BEAN was founded on a simple belief: coffee isn’t just fuel for the day—it’s an intentional ritual that bridges human connection and craftsmanship.

We partner directly with regenerative farming co-ops in Huila, Yirgacheffe, and Antigua. By cutting out brokers and paying up to 140% above fair-trade commodity rates, we ensure farmers thrive while we roast only the top 3% of harvested beans.
